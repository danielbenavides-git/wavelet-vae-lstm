## Role in the project

Each variable maps to a specific stage of the analysis.

**Primary input (CWT scalograms)**
`precio_bolsa_horario.csv` is the only input to the wavelet transform. Hourly spot prices are deflated to real pesos (`precio_bolsa_real.csv`) and segmented into non-overlapping daily windows, with or without a log transform (see Scalograms below). Each day's mean is removed before the CWT, so the scalograms carry only the intraday shape and become inputs to the VAE. The daily mean and standard deviation are kept as separate level variables.

**Regime validation (Interpretation and Validation)**
Once the VAE latent space is clustered into regimes, the following variables help identify what each regime represents:

- `aportes_energia_diario.csv` and `porcentaje_volumen_util_diario.csv` - direct proxies for hydrological stress. Low inflows and low reservoir levels correlate with El Nino episodes (2002-03, 2009-10, 2015-16, 2023-24) and historically precede price spikes.
- `generacion_real_horaria.csv` - reveals the generation mix shift. When hydro capacity drops, thermal plants increase output, which raises marginal costs and spot prices.
- `demanda_real_horaria.csv` and `demanda_sin_diaria.csv` - context for distinguishing price increases driven by supply constraints (regime-relevant) from those driven by demand growth (less relevant).
- `precio_bolsa_diario_ponderado.csv` - smoothed daily reference for quick time-series plots and correlation analysis.

**Limited coverage**
- `precio_escasez_superior.csv` covers only 2025-03 onward. Not usable for historical validation. Included for completeness.
- `max_precio_oferta_horario.csv` starts 2009-08. Usable for the second half of the dataset.

## Scalograms

Generated by `scripts/04_generate_scalograms.py --price {log,linear} [--window {day,week,calweek}]` in `data/processed/scalograms/`. All sets use Morlet `cmor1.5-1.0` with scales 1 to 128. The daily and weekly sets have one scalogram per day. The daily sets (`--window day`, the default) have 9,709 scalograms of 128 scales x 24 hours with each day's mean removed. The weekly sets (`--window week`) have 9,703 scalograms of 128 scales x 168 hours: the window of day t is day t and the 6 days before it, it moves one day at a time, the mean of the 7-day window is removed, and the first 6 days have no scalogram. The calendar week set (`--window calweek`) has 1,386 scalograms of 128 scales x 168 hours, one per week from Monday 00:00 to Sunday 23:00 (2000-01-03 to 2026-07-26), with no overlap and the mean of the week removed. The daily sets differ only in the log transform.

| Folder | `--price` | Series before the CWT | Level columns in metadata |
|---|---|---|---|
| `real_price_log/` | `log` | log real price, daily mean removed | `log_price_mean`, `log_price_std` |
| `real_price/` | `linear` | real price in COP/kWh, daily mean removed (archived on 2026-10-29, not used) | `price_mean`, `price_std` |
| `real_price_log_weekly/` | `log --window week` | log real price, 7-day window mean removed | `log_price_mean`, `log_price_std` (of day t) |
| `real_price_log_calweek/` | `log --window calweek` | log real price, mean of the week removed | `log_price_mean`, `log_price_std` (of the week) |

Files in each folder:

| File | In git | Content |
|---|---|---|
| `scalograms.npy` | No (about 114 MB daily, 796 MB weekly and 114 MB calendar week, regenerate with the script) | float32 array (9709, 128, 24) daily, (9703, 128, 168) weekly or (1386, 128, 168) calendar week, CWT magnitude scaled to [0, 1] with the global minimum and the set's 99.5th percentile, values above clipped |
| `scalogram_metadata.csv` | Yes | `window_id`, `start_datetime`, `end_datetime` (first and last hour of day t) and the two daily level columns of the set; weekly sets also have `window_start_datetime` (first hour of the window). In the calendar week set each row is a week: `start_datetime` and `end_datetime` are Monday 00:00 and Sunday 23:00, the level columns are of the whole week, and `unit` = week |
| `normalization_params.json` | Yes | Pre-CWT transform (`price_set`, `log_transform`, `daily_demean`, `window_demean` in weekly and calendar week sets, and `calendar_weeks` in the calendar week set), scaling (`global_min`, `upper_percentile`, `upper_value`, `clipped_fraction`), wavelet, scales and window |

## El Nino price statistics

`data/processed/NOAA/el_nino_price_stats.csv`, generated by `scripts/05_el_nino_price_stats.py` from `precio_bolsa_diario_real.csv` and `oni.csv`. A day is El Nino when the ONI of its month is >= 0.5. Each row is one group of El Nino days, and the reference (`rest_`) is every day that is not El Nino. Prices are daily real prices in COP/kWh.

| Column | Content |
|---|---|
| `group`, `type` | Name and kind of the group: `thesis episode` (2015-2016 and 2023-2024), `consecutive El Nino months` (each episode in the data) or `all` (all El Nino days) |
| `first_day`, `last_day`, `days` | First and last day of the group and its number of days |
| `median_price`, `mean_price`, `std_price`, `p10_price`, `p90_price` | Price of the group's days |
| `rest_days`, `rest_median_price`, `rest_mean_price`, `rest_std_price` | The same for the days that are not El Nino |
| `median_ratio_to_rest` | `median_price` / `rest_median_price` |
| `pct_above_rest_median`, `pct_above_rest_mean`, `pct_above_rest_mean_plus_1std` | % of the group's days above the median, the mean and the mean + 1 std of the other days |
| `pct_above_all_days_median` | % of the group's days above the median of all days |

## Notes

- Hourly files encode hours as columns (`Values_Hour01`..`Values_Hour24`), not rows, subsequet data processing follows in the project
- Raw monetary values are in Colombian pesos (COP) per kWh, nominal. Real prices (`precio_bolsa_real.csv`, `precio_bolsa_diario_real.csv`) are deflated with the chained DANE IPC (base January 2000 = 100)
- The API returns at most 30 days per request for hourly variables. `pydataxm` handles this internally
